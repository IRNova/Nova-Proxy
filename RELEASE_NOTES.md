# Nova Proxy 4.9.2

A Farsi wording fix in the panel. Nothing behaves differently.

## What changed

4.9.1 added a **Second address: Pages project** setting in Settings → System extras. Its Farsi help text was written in translated English rather than in Persian: it addressed the reader informally while the rest of the panel addresses them formally, and it transliterated two words Persian already has.

The text now reads as Persian. The English and Russian versions are unchanged, and so is everything the setting does.

## Should you update

If you do not read Farsi, this release changes nothing for you and 4.9.1 is fine to stay on.

## Updating

Panels do not update themselves. Use the update button in your panel, or the Update option in the Telegram bot. Your users, settings and data are kept. After updating, your panel should report **4.9.2**.
